02 / How I work

Modern tools.
Accountable engineering.

AI is part of how I build. Architecture, product decisions, and the quality of what ships remain my responsibility.

01

Understand the product.

Start with the people, the workflow, and the problem. Turn that understanding into a clear scope and practical technical decisions.

02

Own the whole system.

Connect data models, backend services, interfaces, integrations, and deployment. Keep the code organized and the next change manageable.

03

Use AI. Apply judgment.

I work with Codex, OpenAI, and Google AI alongside RubyMine. I review the output and take responsibility for how it fits the product.

04

Validate before release.

Code review, automated tests, browser QA, security checks, and staging validation are part of my delivery workflow.

A practical point of view

Languages evolve.
Engineering judgment matters.

I choose languages and tools to suit the product. Across Ruby, Python, and JavaScript, my focus is understanding the problem, shaping the architecture, and delivering software that people can rely on.

As coding agents take on more implementation work, I stay responsible for the decisions, validation, and quality of what ships.
